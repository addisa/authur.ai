What he does

Small things, done right, every day.

Authur isn't trying to be everything. He's trying to be the one buddy who keeps your week from quietly falling apart — by handling the dozen small chores that pile up while you're focused on real work.

i.

Reads your inbox

Triages email, surfaces what actually needs you, drafts replies in your voice, and quietly archives the rest.

"4 things need your eyes today. The other 23 can wait."
ii.

Keeps the to-do list

Captures every "I should remember to…", sorts by what's urgent vs. important, and nudges only when nudging helps.

"Three carry-overs from yesterday. Want me to bump one?"
iii.

Watches the clock

Holds your calendar, remembers the meeting before the meeting, and gives you a real heads-up — not a panic ping.

"Coffee with Sam in 25 — your notes from last time are open."
iv.

Answers the small Qs

"What was that restaurant we liked?" "When's mom's birthday?" "Is that flight delayed?" — fast, honest, and sourced.

"Pasta place near the park — Mona. Open till 10."
v.

Remembers, gently

Names of your friends' kids. Your sister's coffee order. The thing you said last Tuesday you'd circle back on. The little stuff.

"Lila turns 7 next Friday — last year you sent the dino book."
vi.

Drafts the boring stuff

The reschedule email. The dentist confirmation. The "circling back" follow-up. Written like you'd write it — only faster.

"Draft ready. Tone: warm but firm. Hit send?"
How he works

Authur is a HERMES agent.

Hermes Agent, by Nous Research, is the open framework Authur runs on. It's a simple idea: an AI that can actually do things — read your email, edit a doc, set a reminder — instead of just talking about doing them. Hermes is what reaches out and gets the job done.

Most AI you've met can chat. Authur can act. Hermes gives him a small set of trustworthy hands — one for your inbox, one for your calendar, one for your notes — and a clear rulebook for using them.

C

Connected

He plugs into the tools you already use — your email, your calendar, your notes app. No new dashboard to learn.

L

Local-first

Your data stays yours. Authur reads what he needs, when he needs it, and nothing more.

A

Asks first

Before he sends, deletes, or schedules anything that touches another person, he checks with you.

W

Watchable

Every action he takes shows up in his daily log (see below). Nothing happens in secret.
